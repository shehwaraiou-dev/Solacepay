How it works

Three steps. One afternoon.
A payroll system that scales for a decade.

Most teams are running their first global payroll the same day they sign up. No long sales call required — but we'll happily show up if you'd like a walkthrough.

Run my first payroll
  1. 01

    Invite your team.

    Send a single magic link — contractors and full-time staff onboard themselves, including tax forms, wallet, and local payout preferences.

  2. 02

    Fund your treasury.

    Wire from your bank, swap from your existing wallet, or top up with a card. Stablecoins land in seconds and are yours to disburse, not ours to hold.

  3. 03

    Run payroll. Sleep well.

    Approve once. Everyone gets paid before you've refreshed the page. Audit log is signed, sealed, and exportable to your accounting stack.
